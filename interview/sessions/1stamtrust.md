# [First American Trust](https://www.firstamtrust.com/) - 2nd Interview
> with [Srinivas Sampige](https://www.linkedin.com/in/srinivas-sampige-39baa02/)

## In addition to the technical skills and experience, other characteristics Srinivas is looking for include:

* The ability to multitask, think on your feet and make decisions.
* Interest in tackling the most significant production issues.
* The ability to interact with executives and stakeholders and be able to propose and whiteboard a solution in executive meetings.
* Ability to architect/design solutions and then build consensus amongst all parties.
* The ability to serve as the go-to-support person for Senior Engineers (to solve the toughest problems).

---

## Questions

1. Without revealing anything proprietary, given the high fiduciary nature of the trust/banking/title business, how do you uniquely protect your software systems from both idempotent and non-idempotent attacks? <span class=answer>This question is for the architect</span>
1. How quickly can transactions be settled using Xpresswire once all parties have approved their interest in the transaction? Is this speed based on in-house developed solutions or by more heavily leveraging 3rd party applications/solutions? Has this speed changed since the announcement of FedNow in April or its official rollout in July? <span class=answer>This is a business-level question and would need to be answered by management, if agreed to.</span>
1. Has First American Trust developed any products or services that are leveraged by other firms in or outside the financial services industry? <span class=answer>Srinivas doesn't think so , but this is also an architect question.</span>
1. Can you briefly cover the role of Midas within First American Trust and what its most immediate needs are, whether
   enhancements, greenfield development, or direct change requests from management? <span class=answer>Midas was
   greenfield when it was first developed and is constantly receiving change requests which is what the team I would be
   joining is working on.</span>
1. Is Midas split up by domain, or is it more of a monolith application/service? If it's a monolith, will this role be
   tasked with splitting it up into domains/microservices? <span class=answer>Midas is used to to facilitate
   Xpresswire, for example. Midas is a monolith currently, but is likely to be moved into a more domain-based architecture and could be in the list of duties for this role.</span>
1. Is Midas only a headless, backend service or is there a frontend? If not, are there any plans for development of a
   frontend interface? <span class=answer>Midas is headless only and there are no plans for a frontend</span>
1. How senior is the next most senior developer on the Midas team? <span class=answer>2 devs have 5 years and 2 have 2.
   A couple started with .NET C#</span>
