About The Role

Responsibilities
- Design data contracts and processing semantics for identity, eligibility, audience attributes, deduplication, and field persistence
- Build end-to-end observability and reconciliation across the data lifecycle
- Lead technical discovery across Member Marketing and Coverage Eligibility
- Design for correctness and auditability in regulated workflows (provenance, consent, suppression, rollback, replay)
- Modernize file-based processes toward well-contracted, event-driven integrations
- Apply AI-augmented tooling where it improves velocity without compromising correctness
